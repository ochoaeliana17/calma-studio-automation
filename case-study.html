01 — The Problem

A 2-person team drowning in content tasks

Calma Studio serves 3,000+ users across Spain, Portugal, LATAM, and the UK. Their content team was spending 12+ hours every week writing social media posts manually — in three languages, across three platforms, with no consistent tone and no editorial calendar.

LinkedIn had been completely abandoned. Instagram was inconsistent. The team had no time left for strategy, community engagement, or growth.

03 — The Solution

An AI content system built on Claude, Zapier and Notion

The system works in one direction: the team inputs a wellness topic, and the automation handles everything else — generation, translation, scheduling, and calendar logging.

1

Topic input in Notion

Team fills in a Notion database row: topic, platform, language, publish date. When status changes to "Ready", Zapier triggers automatically.

2

Zapier selects the right prompt

Based on platform + language combination, Zapier selects the correct Claude prompt template (6 prompts total: Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook × ES/PT/EN).

3

Claude generates the content

Claude API receives the prompt with the topic filled in and returns platform-specific, brand-consistent copy in the correct language and tone.

4

Team reviews in Notion

Generated content is written back to Notion for a quick human review. Status updates to "Approved" when ready to publish.

5

Zapier publishes automatically

On approval, Zapier publishes to the correct platform via API. Notion calendar is updated automatically with publish date and status.

Prompt system

6 Claude prompts — one for each platform and language

Each prompt is engineered to match the platform format, audience, and brand voice — Instagram captions feel different from LinkedIn posts, and Spanish content is adapted culturally, not just translated.
